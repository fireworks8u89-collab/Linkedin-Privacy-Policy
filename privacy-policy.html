Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the n8n LinkedIn Automation application handles information when it is used to connect LinkedIn with an n8n automation workflow.

1. Purpose of the Application

This application is a personal automation project used to connect LinkedIn with n8n for creating and managing LinkedIn content through authorized automation workflows.

2. Information We Access

When you authorize this application, LinkedIn may provide information necessary to authenticate your LinkedIn account and perform the LinkedIn actions that you have authorized.

The application only requests and uses information necessary for its intended LinkedIn automation functions.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through LinkedIn is used only to operate the authorized n8n automation, including authentication and publishing LinkedIn content when the workflow is configured to do so.

4. Data Storage

This application is intended for personal use. The application owner does not intentionally sell, rent, or share LinkedIn information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data may be processed by the n8n instance and by third-party services explicitly configured in the automation workflow, such as an AI service used to generate content. Such services are used only as part of the configured workflow.

5. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect application credentials and information processed by the automation. Users should not share OAuth client secrets, access tokens, or other authentication credentials with unauthorized people.

6. Third-Party Services

The automation may interact with LinkedIn, n8n, and other services chosen by the application owner. Each service may have its own privacy policy and terms governing information processed through that service.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the automation and maintain its configuration, unless a longer retention period is required by law or by the policies of a connected service.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
